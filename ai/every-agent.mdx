## Overview

You work with Every in chat. Instead of clicking through menus, you describe what you need in plain language and Every creates, updates, analyzes, and drafts across your workspace.

It is not a separate chatbot with a copied database. It works against the live records in your Every workspace and the connected services you authorize.

**What makes it useful:**
- It works directly with your live business data in Every
- It uses your saved [Business DNA and Voice & Tone](/ai/context-and-voice) so its work fits your business and sounds like you
- It [remembers facts and applies skills](/ai/memory-and-skills) you've taught it across every chat
- It connects to external systems like Google Workspace, [LinkedIn and Instagram](/integrations/linkedin-and-instagram), Xero, HubSpot, and Granola
- It handles multi-step workflows without you mapping every click

## Where to find Every

- **Chat** — the **Chat** page in the left nav. Your chats are listed at the left; on a phone, the list is the Chat page and a chat opens from it. Select **New chat** (Shift+Cmd+J) to start a new one.
- **Ask bar** — on other pages, type in **Ask Every…** at the bottom of the page. Every works in the background and tells you when the reply is ready; the chat is on the Chat page. Cmd+J puts your cursor there.
- **Cmd+K** — search your records, start an action, or ask Every from the palette.
- **On a record** — select **Ask Every** on a record card to ask about that record.
- **Other apps** — Slack, ChatGPT, Claude, the iPhone app, email (`agent@every.ai`), and the Every CLI. Find them in **Settings → Apps**.

When a reply names a record, open it and it shows as a side panel over the chat, so you keep your place.

Each chat has a **⋯** menu to rename it, copy a link or the chat as Markdown, or delete it. In a team workspace, the same menu makes a chat private or shares it with your team. See [Private chats](/teams/teams#private-chats).

## What Every Can Reach

Every works directly with your workspace data. It can read, create, update, and (with confirmation) delete across:

| Area | What Every can do |
|-|-|
| **Companies** | Create and update companies, enrich company records, analyze concentration and revenue |
| **People** | Create and affiliate people; import from Google, LinkedIn, or CSV; sync your connected LinkedIn connections and Instagram following; bill a person directly with no company required |
| **Invoices** | Draft, edit, apply tax and discounts, schedule sends, send to clients |
| **Proposals** | Draft structured proposals, track approval, convert issued or approved proposals into invoices |
| **Payments** | Record manual payments, send reminders and confirmations, check Stripe-linked state |
| **Expenses** | Parse receipt uploads into expenses, add unbilled expenses to invoices |
| **Time** | Log time entries, add unbilled time to invoices |
| **Pipeline** | Find prospects, review deals, update sales plans, draft outreach, read your synced LinkedIn and Instagram conversations, and turn a deal's [Auto mode](/prospecting/auto-mode) on or off |
| **Recurring invoices** | Create, pause, and update recurring invoice automations; review run history |

It also runs reporting and analysis on top of all of this — revenue summaries, overdue and cash-flow reviews, client concentration, and service or product trends.

The exact toolset changes as Every adds capabilities. If a task is outside the current product or requires access you have not connected, Every should say so instead of pretending it completed the work.

## What kind of CRM is Every?

Every is an **AI-native CRM and client-operations workspace** for service businesses. It connects relationship-based prospecting and a sales pipeline to proposals, invoices, payments, expenses, time tracking, and your network of people and companies.

It is not a full enterprise CRM administration suite, and it is not a general ledger or tax-filing system. If you already use HubSpot or Xero, Every can work with those connected systems and still handle the client workflows that live in its own workspace.

The public [AI business assistant guide](https://every.ai/ai-business-assistant/) explains how to evaluate assistants by what they can access, what they can change, and where human review remains required. See [Every's AI CRM guide](https://every.ai/ai-crm/) for the connected CRM, pipeline, and client-operations model.

## How Every Works

Every operates in **direct execution mode**: you describe the task, Every figures out the steps, performs them, and shows or confirms the result. You keep refining conversationally.

```text
You: "Create an invoice for ACME Corp for $5,000"
Every: [Shows draft]
You: "Actually make it $5,500 and add a thank-you note"
Every: [Updates the draft]
You: "Perfect, send it"
```

### Confirm Before It Sends or Deletes

In interactive app chat, actions that **leave your workspace or remove data are gated**. Every shows you what it's about to do and waits for your go-ahead before:

- **Sending** anything to a client — invoices, proposals, payment reminders, confirmations, emails, and LinkedIn or Instagram messages
- **Deleting** records — people, companies, invoices, proposals, payments, services, and more

Reads, drafts, and internal edits happen directly so the chat stays fast; the irreversible, client-facing steps are the ones that ask first.

Every request that waits for you, from your chats and from connected apps, is also listed on [**Chat → Approvals**](https://app.every.ai/chat/approvals). Approve or cancel each one, or use **Approve all** or **Cancel all**.

<Warning>
  Always double-check invoice and email details before confirming a send. Once an email is delivered to a client, it cannot be unsent.
</Warning>

Scheduled and background routines use a stricter boundary: they can read, analyze, create internal drafts, and prepare work, but outbound sends and destructive actions are unavailable while nobody is present to review them.

The one exception is a deal where you turned on [Auto mode](/prospecting/auto-mode). You grant it per deal, and only the deal owner can turn it on. With it on, Every replies to and follows up with that deal's contact on its own, over Gmail, LinkedIn, or Instagram, inside conversations that already exist. Each message waits a few minutes before it sends so you can cancel it, and everything it sends appears in the deal's Activity.

### Using Every by email

Email `agent@every.ai` from the address on your Every account to continue a chat from your inbox.

<Card title="Email Every" icon="mail" href="mailto:agent@every.ai">
  Start a request from your inbox
</Card>

Email is a delayed channel, so it uses a different approval flow from interactive chat: instead of an Approve button, you **reply with an exact command**. This covers **outbound sends** (emails, invoices, proposals — Every replies with the send details plus a one-time `SEND <code>` command) and **invoice deletion**, currently the only destructive email action. For deletion, ask naturally—such as “Delete invoice INV-123”—and Every emails back the invoice details plus the exact command:

```text
DELETE INVOICE INV-123
```

Reply directly to that confirmation email with only the command to approve. To cancel, reply with:

```text
CANCEL DELETE INVOICE INV-123
```

The newly written part of your reply must be exact, the invoice number must match literally, and the approval expires after 24 hours. Standard quoted reply history is ignored, but extra prose or a signature will not approve the deletion.

## Navigation

You don't have to know where a page lives. Ask Every to take you somewhere and it can navigate the app for you — "open the payments page," "take me to my overdue invoices," "show me ACME's company record." This pairs well with analysis: ask a question, then jump straight to the records behind the answer.

## In the message box

- **+** adds context (a record or a skill), attaches files, or adds files from Google Drive. You can also paste or drop a file.
- **Mode** (in the **+** menu) switches between **Auto**, which adjusts to each request, and **Max**, for the hardest tasks.
- Type **@** to tag a record or an active skill.
- While Every is working, you can keep typing. New messages wait in a queue and go out in order.

## Voice Input

Every supports voice input. Click the microphone icon in the message box and speak your request. Select the check mark to stop: your words go into the message box, so you can review them before you send.

**Useful for:**
- Logging time while working
- Capturing expenses quickly
- Sending short requests from mobile

### Rules

Beyond freeform memory, Every can save specific, standing business rules.

**Examples:**
- "ACME Corp always gets Net 15 terms"
- "Never apply tax to consulting services"
- "Enterprise clients default to 45-day terms"
- "Always include project reference numbers on TechStart invoices"

When Every proposes saving a rule, it asks you to confirm. Manage saved rules in [**Business DNA → Business Rules**](https://app.every.ai/agent/rules).

<Note>
  Rules are standing guidance and preferences for how Every works across your tasks. Skills are reusable procedures for specific tasks. [Agent Memory](/ai/memory-and-skills) is freeform recall (running lists, observations, open todos). [Business DNA](/ai/context-and-voice) is the structured profile of your business. They work together.
</Note>

**Supported file types include:** PDF, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, CSV files, images, and plain text files.

### Moving from another CRM or invoicing tool

Attach a representative CSV or XLSX file in a private chat on the [Chat](https://app.every.ai/chat) page. Specify the records you want to import. Review the proposed fields and record matches before you confirm. Check the receipt and verify a sample of the imported records.

### Download a financial CSV

In a private chat, request a CSV of active invoice summaries or active expenses. Include a date range if needed. Keep file requests that contain workspace data in private chat.

## Batch & Multi-Step Workflows

Every can apply changes across many records at once, and break a goal into ordered steps.

```text
"Update all enterprise clients to Net 45"
"Archive clients with no invoice activity in the last 2 years"
"Change tax rate to 13% on all consulting services"
```

```text
"Set up my consulting business for 2026:
1. Create a new service for strategy work at $175/hour
2. Update my recurring automations to use the new rate
3. Draft a rate change note for retainer clients"
```

## Undoing Actions by Every

Every includes a built-in undo system. For supported changes, an **Undo** button appears with Every’s action summary so you can review and reverse the recorded changes.

### How It Works

- An **Undo** button appears when a recorded undo is available
- Hover to preview which items will be affected (they highlight in yellow)
- Click to reverse **all actions from that agent turn** (it's all-or-nothing to prevent inconsistent states)
- You can also undo from **Activity** on [Home](https://app.every.ai/)

### What Can Be Undone

Most data operations can be reversed:

- **Invoices & proposals** — creation, updates, status changes, line item additions
- **Payments & time tracking** — payment records, time entries, expense records
- **People & Companies** — supported record changes for either party type
- **Complex operations** — multi-step workflows, bulk operations, CSV import results

### What Cannot Be Undone

- **Emails already sent** to clients (invoices, notifications)
- **Third-party actions** already processed (Stripe payments, Google Calendar events, HubSpot syncs)
- **Unrecorded operations** — not every tool or external service action has an undo snapshot

### Limitations

- The ordinary chat undo buffer holds the **latest recorded data-changing turn** for the workspace. A newer recorded change replaces it; a read-only reply does not.
- After undoing, you cannot "redo" — ask Every again instead
- Undo can replace later manual edits to affected records. Review the actions before confirming; do not rely on automatic conflict detection to preserve those edits.

<Tip>
  If you notice an error, undo immediately before Every performs more actions. It is easier to undo right away than after additional turns.
</Tip>

## Activity History

Each action that Every takes is recorded in **Activity** on [Home](https://app.every.ai/). Use it to review what Every did, see which records changed, and use an available undo action. Activity history does not make every older turn undoable.

## Data access and AI limits

Every scopes workspace data to your organization. Connected services use the permissions you grant; for example, Google Drive uses file-by-file access rather than permission to browse your full Drive. Some AI features send the content and context needed for the request to Every's AI service providers, as described in the [Privacy Policy](https://every.ai/privacy).

AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Use the source records, previews, and permission cards to verify consequential work, especially recipient addresses, proposal scope, invoice amounts, payment records, and imported data.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Every send an email or invoice without me reviewing it?">
    In interactive chat, outbound send tools pause on an approval card showing what will be sent. Scheduled and unattended routines can prepare drafts but cannot perform the final send by themselves. The exception is a deal where you turned on [Auto mode](/prospecting/auto-mode): there, Every replies and follows up with the deal's contact on its own, with a few minutes to cancel each message.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Every replace an accounting system?">
    Every handles proposals, invoices, payments, expenses, and time tracking. Use your accounting system for ledger, reconciliation, payroll, and tax work. The [Xero connection](/integrations/xero) gives Every access to supported Xero records and actions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a connection give Every access to everything in that service?">
    No. Each connection has a defined permission scope. For example, Google Drive access is limited to files you explicitly select or create with Every. Review the permissions during setup and see the [Privacy Policy](https://every.ai/privacy) for current processing and provider terms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I migrate from another CRM?">
    Yes. Attach a representative CSV or XLSX file in a private chat. Review the mapping before you confirm. Check the receipt and verify a sample of records.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
